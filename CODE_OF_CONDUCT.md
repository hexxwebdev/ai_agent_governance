# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to fostering a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus discussions on improving the project.
- Provide helpful feedback.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or abusive language.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
- Spam or malicious content.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may edit, remove, or moderate content that violates these guidelines and may restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please open a private security report or contact the project maintainers.
